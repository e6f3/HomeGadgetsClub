The Shed Society

We’re a club for people whose yards are full of clever things. Every month we haul one outdoor gadget onto the lawn, test it in real weather, and decide whether it earns a spot by the back door.

When we meet

Everyone brings a gadget and a folding chair. Rain moves us onto the porch.

First Saturday
Show & Grill
Members demo what they bought, and what they regret buying, over burgers.
Third Sunday
The Teardown
We open one device on a picnic blanket and name every part we can.
Last Thursday
Porch Court
Weather trials. Gadgets that fail in the first drizzle go back to the shed.

Join the shed

Dues are one working gadget per year and a story about why you bought it. Leave your email and we’ll send the next backyard’s address.
